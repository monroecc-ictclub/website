TBA Understanding Ethernet Cables

Learn the fundamentals of Ethernet cabling and discover why different cable types matter in computer networking. During this hands-on workshop, students will explore the differences between UTP (Unshielded Twisted Pair) and STP (Shielded Twisted Pair) cables, understand Ethernet cable categories (Cat5e, Cat6, Cat6a, and beyond), and learn when each type is used.

Participants will also gain practical experience by creating their own Ethernet patch cable using professional networking tools. You'll learn how to correctly arrange conductors, terminate RJ-45 connectors, and test your finished cable to ensure it works properly.

What We'll Discuss

  • How Ethernet cables transmit network data
  • The differences between UTP and STP cabling
  • Understanding Ethernet cable categories (Cat5e, Cat6, Cat6a, and more)
  • When and why different cable types are used
  • How to build and terminate an Ethernet cable
  • Testing a completed cable for proper connectivity
