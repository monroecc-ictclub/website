Club Projects

Learning by Building

Student Projects

One of the best ways to learn technology is by building real-world projects. The Information & Computer Technology Club encourages students to collaborate on software, hardware, networking, cybersecurity, Linux, and web development projects throughout the academic year. As projects are completed, they will be showcased here to inspire future members and highlight the work of the club.

PC Building

Artemis is building a personal computer! Our PC building workshop helped clarify the design choices, and an upcoming workshop will help put it all together. Pictures of the completed build are coming soon!

Cyberdeck Laptop

Collin is building a cyberdeck, a custom portable computer built from scratch. The build pairs a Raspberry Pi 3 Model B+ with a 7-inch touchscreen and a mini keyboard with a built-in touchpad. Powered with battery banks with plans for a 3D printed chassis in progress!

Pictures of the build are coming soon, so check back as it comes together!

Club Website

The website you're reading right now is a club project! Since September, club leadership has built and maintained it by hand with HTML and CSS, adding the events calendar, office hours, workshop guides, and this student project showcase.

The site's code lives on GitHub, so it's a great way to put what you learned in Git & GitHub 101 into practice. Members can contribute anything from fixing a typo to designing a new page. Talk to an officer if you'd like to help!
